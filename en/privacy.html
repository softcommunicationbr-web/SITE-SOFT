Who is responsible for your data

Soft Communication, Résidence Bel Air View, Pamatai, 98704 Faa’a, Tahiti, French Polynesia.

Data controller: Calebe · Contact: soft.communicationbr@gmail.com · +689 87 04 31 12.

The data we receive

  • Contact form: your name, email, company or brand, industry sector, WhatsApp number, project summary, and desired timeline.
  • Direct correspondence: information and details shared voluntarily when communicating via WhatsApp, email, or telephone.
  • Call bookings: name, email address, and selected time slot via our online scheduling module (Cal.com) or direct messaging.
  • Browsing telemetry: anonymized technical audience metrics, collected only after your express consent. See our cookie policy.

We do not collect sensitive personal data or financial payment information on this website.

Why we use it

  • To address your enquiries and formulate tailored production quotes.
  • To schedule introductory discovery calls and consultations.
  • To maintain seamless business and artistic communications.
  • To analyze general website usage trends and improve usability, subject to your consent.

These activities are based on steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract, on your express consent, or on our legitimate business interest in responding to enquiries.

Who can access it

Your personal data is solely intended for Soft Communication. We never sell, lease, or trade your personal information to third parties.

It may be processed confidentially by our trusted technical partners strictly within the scope of their assigned services:

  • Web infrastructure hosting: Hostinger.
  • Communications: WhatsApp Business and Google Workspace mail.
  • Online scheduling: Cal.com (secure booking integration).
  • Audience measurement & social: privacy-focused analytics and Meta advertising pixels, enabled strictly upon explicit opt-in.

Our providers comply with European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) and international data protection standards aligned with GDPR.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and quotes: 3 years from the date of the last commercial contact.
  • Client accounts and contracts: 5 years from project delivery, pursuant to statutory legal and accounting obligations.
  • Analytics cookies and telemetry: 6 to 13 months maximum from initial placement.

Your rights

In accordance with data protection regulations applicable in French Polynesia and the amended French Data Protection Act of 6 January 1978, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and object to the processing of your personal data.

You can exercise these rights at any time by sending a message to: soft.communicationbr@gmail.com. We will respond promptly.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you are entitled to file a complaint with the French Data Protection Authority (CNIL, cnil.fr).

Security

We enforce rigorous technical and organizational safeguards to ensure the security, integrity, and confidentiality of your information against unauthorized access.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect evolving practices or legal requirements. The latest revision date is displayed at the top of the page.
