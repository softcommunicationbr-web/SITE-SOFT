What a cookie is

A cookie is a compact text file stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when visiting a website. It allows the site to remember your preferences and ensure smooth navigation.

Your choice

Upon your first visit, a privacy banner invites you to either accept or decline non-essential cookies. Both choices carry equal prominence and are equally straightforward.

Until you provide your explicit consent, no audience measurement or advertising cookies are deployed. Choosing to decline will never restrict your ability to browse our website.

The cookies we use

  • Essential (Technical): necessary for core website functions and for remembering your privacy choices. These do not require prior consent.
  • Audience measurement: privacy-preserving tools designed to assess aggregate traffic and identify the most viewed sections anonymously.
  • Advertising & social: campaign pixels (Meta / Instagram), enabled only with your explicit permission to evaluate campaign effectiveness.
  • Online booking: functional cookies deployed by Cal.com to manage and hold your selected discovery call slot.

Detailed technical specifications of active trackers remain available upon request.

Duration

Your preference choice (acceptance or refusal) is retained on your device for up to 6 months. After this time, the consent banner will be presented again.
