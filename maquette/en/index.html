Everything you need to know before starting our collaboration.

How are shoots organized across the islands (Bora Bora, Moorea, etc.)? ▼
We are based in French Polynesia and travel regularly to Tahiti, Moorea, Bora Bora, Raiatea, and the Tuamotu archipelago. We handle all technical logistics so you don't have to worry about travel coordination.
What are the turnaround times for delivery? ▼
Following the shoot session, your initial fully edited videos are delivered within 14 business days. You then get instant access to request adjustments with just a few clicks.
Are full commercial usage rights included? ▼
Yes, absolutely. All delivered content includes complete global commercial usage rights (organic social, website, Meta, TikTok, YouTube ads) with zero recurring royalties.
Can we request revisions? ▼
Yes! All our packages include unlimited revisions. We fine-tune the pace, music, captions, and cuts until you are 100% thrilled with the final result.
Are the videos optimized for paid advertising? ▼
Every video is scripted with scientifically tested hook techniques to stop the scroll and drive action, driving down cost-per-click and maximizing ROI.
